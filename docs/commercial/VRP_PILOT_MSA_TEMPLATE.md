## 1. Purpose

This Agreement establishes the general terms under which Provider may perform controlled VRP Pilot services for Participant.

Specific services, environments, Deliverables, milestones, schedules, responsibilities, Acceptance Criteria, and Fees must be defined in a participant-specific Statement of Work signed by both Parties.

This Agreement does not require either Party to enter into a Statement of Work.

Pilot activity begins only after:

- the applicable Statement of Work has been signed;
- required admission checks have been completed;
- required security conditions have been satisfied;
- required initial payment has been received;
- Provider has issued written activation confirmation.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Acceptance Criteria

The objective criteria stated in a Statement of Work for determining whether a Deliverable conforms to the agreed requirements.

### 2.2 Affiliate

The systems, infrastructure, networks, regions, workloads, accounts, and deployment boundaries expressly approved in a Statement of Work.

### 2.4 Authorised Personnel

Individuals expressly permitted to access Pilot Materials or participate in Pilot activity.

### 2.5 Background Technology

Technology, software, protocols, documentation, methods, designs, inventions, know-how, trade secrets, tools, and intellectual property developed, acquired, or controlled independently of the applicable Statement of Work.

### 2.6 Confidential Information

Non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party that is identified as confidential or should reasonably be understood to be confidential.

### 2.7 Deliverable

An item expressly identified as a Deliverable in a signed Statement of Work.

### 2.8 Evidence Output

An approved record, manifest, verification result, report, measurement, or other output produced during the Pilot.

### 2.9 Fees

Amounts payable under a signed Statement of Work.

### 2.10 Participant Data

Data, configurations, records, or materials provided by or on behalf of Participant.

### 2.11 Pilot

A limited and controlled evaluation of specified VRP capabilities under a signed Statement of Work.

### 2.12 Pilot Materials

Approved software, interfaces, configurations, credentials, Documentation, Evidence Output, reports, or other materials made available for the Pilot.

### 2.13 Protected Technology

Provider’s non-public Background Technology, including protected VRP runtime technology, source code, protocol implementation, algorithms, security mechanisms, internal state models, cryptographic implementation, build systems, and proprietary operational methods.

### 2.14 Services

Engineering, integration, validation, reporting, support, or related services described in a Statement of Work.

### 2.15 Statement of Work or SOW

A participant-specific document signed by both Parties that incorporates this Agreement and defines a particular engagement.

### 2.16 VRP

The Veil Routing Protocol architecture and its associated continuity, recovery, runtime, validation, evidence, and integration technologies.
